04WHY THIS EXISTS

I read because I enjoy the moment an assumption becomes a question.

This website is a record of that process — notes from books, history, philosophy, religion, politics and whatever else makes me stop and reconsider what I thought I understood.

Not a collection of final answers. A record of changing questions.
